## 🪐 ABOUT THE GAME

**VOIDBOUND: LAST HORIZON** is an independent science-fiction space combat game.

Take control of your spacecraft, prepare for battle, face dangerous enemies and fight your way towards the ultimate confrontation.

The galaxy is on the edge of destruction. Your mission is to reach the Last Horizon.

**The future is in your hands.**
